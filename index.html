Bounds, said plainly. The model computes bounds from vendors’ stated bandwidths and peak rates. For chips that read their weights from DRAM the bound is close to what systems achieve; for SRAM designs the published speeds are a small share of it, and the decks say so. No vendor claim is used as an input.
